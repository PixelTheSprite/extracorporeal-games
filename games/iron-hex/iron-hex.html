Presets

Tested settings from A/B runs of AI-versus-AI battles on 26 × 18 maps. Times assume about 4 seconds per order; your own turns may differ. Picking a preset replaces the rules, victory condition and roster below; change anything afterwards to make it your own.

Looks

Period looks are cosmetic. Your units wear a look once you've unlocked it for that unit type (see the Unit library); AI armies can wear any look.

Starting roster

Every army starts with this roster. Up to 4 of each unit and 16 units in total.

Rules

Supply: flags and captured cities are supply points. At the end of each turn, units standing in friendly territory get +2 ammo and a full fuel tank. Units in neutral or enemy territory get nothing. Planes burn 2 fuel per turn just staying airborne and crash at zero; other units stop moving when their fuel runs out.

Diplomacy

Put armies on teams for this Quick battle. Allies never fight each other and win together; what each army grants its allies can be changed at any time, one-sidedly.

Multiplayer
