How to Play

I

Gather wildflowers

Press Gather Wildflower. The meadow reel spins and settles on the bloom you collect, which is planted in your bed. The bed holds 24 flowers.

II

Tend your bed

Hover over a flower to see its card. Click (or tap) it to pin the card, then choose it for crossing or uproot it.

III

Cross two flowers

Choose two parents. The Crossing Bench shows a Punnett square for each trait (stem, petals and pistil). Press Crossbreed to sprout a seedling, then plant it if you'd like to keep it.

IV

Read the genes

Every trait carries two alleles. Uppercase is dominant, lowercase recessive. One dominant allele is enough to show its colour (Pp blooms crimson). A recessive colour appears only when both alleles are recessive (pp blooms blush pink). Genotypes read stem · petals · pistil.

V

Know your forms

The expressed petal allele decides the species (rose, iris, tulip or poppy). The stem allele decides how the leaves grow. The Herbarium (the i button) lists every allele.

VI

Earn laurels

Milestones in gathering, breeding, lineage and colour earn laurels, announced the moment you earn them. See them all on the Laurels page in the ☰ menu, where you can also save, load or start a new garden.

❧ Your garden is also kept in this browser between visits.
