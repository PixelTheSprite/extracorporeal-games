How to play
  • W/S raise or lower sails (back oars, furled, oars, half, full)
  • A/D turn the rudder
  • Q port broadside, E starboard, Space both, or click the water on either side
  • F drop a supply buoy (max 4)
  • P pause, M sound

The ring on the wind compass shows which headings catch wind: green sails fast, red is in irons. Use oars to crawl upwind or tack across it.

Forts and buoys supply territory around them. Silence an enemy fort with broadsides, then stay close until your flag goes up. Hold 55% of the sea or take every Crown fort to win.
